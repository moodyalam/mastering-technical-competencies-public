## What You'll Learn

By the end of this lecture, you'll be able to:

- Choose between **autocomplete**, **inline chat** and **chat** (Ask or Agent
  mode) for a task, and **check** what the AI produces.
- Follow a **debugging routine**: reproduce, hypothesise, observe, fix, verify.
- Use **logs**, a **conditional breakpoint**, **Variables**, **Watch** and the
  **Debug Console** to find a bug, and a **logpoint** or **`pdb`** when you
  can't pause or have no IDE.

Analogy: recipe and ingredients, and a meal being cooked. You can cook many meals from one recipe; eating one doesn't change the recipe. Students most often confuse the two: "I edited the container, why didn't the image change?" Do NOT say what happens to files here: that is Mentimeter question 1.

Docker Desktop on Mac and Windows quietly runs one small Linux VM underneath, because containers are Linux programs. CS2023 expects students to compare containers and VMs and weigh the trade-offs: weaker isolation than a VM, much lighter.
